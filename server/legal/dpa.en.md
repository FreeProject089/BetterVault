# Data processing agreement (DPA)

In accordance with Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Effective date: {{effectiveDate}}

## 1. Parties

- **The Controller**: the client organisation using the service for its members ("the Client").
- **The Processor**: {{operatorName}}, {{operatorAddress}}, contact {{contactEmail}} ("the Operator").

This agreement supplements the [terms of use](/legal/terms?lang=en) and prevails over them for everything related to personal data.

## 2. Subject and duration

The Operator hosts and syncs the Client's encrypted vaults for as long as the service is used.

## 3. Nature and purposes of processing

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Nature | Storage, sync, sharing and backup of end-to-end encrypted data |
| Purpose | Providing the BetterVault password and task manager |
| Categories of data subjects | Members, employees and contractors of the Client who have an account |
| Categories of data | Email, session metadata (truncated IP{{#si geo}}, approximate location{{/si}}, device), encrypted content the Operator cannot read |
| Sensitive data | None accessible to the Operator: all content is encrypted with keys it does not hold |

## 4. Operator obligations

The Operator:

1. only processes data on the Client's documented instructions, these terms being those instructions;
2. ensures that people authorised to administer the server are bound by confidentiality;
3. implements the security measures in the annex ([Security measures](/legal/security?lang=en));
4. only engages another processor after informing the Client, who may object ([current list](/legal/subprocessors?lang=en));
5. helps the Client respond to data subject requests, in particular through the export and account deletion features;
6. helps the Client ensure security, notify breaches and carry out an impact assessment where needed;
7. notifies the Client of any personal data breach **within 72 hours** of becoming aware of it, with the information available;
8. deletes the data when the service ends: immediately on the server{{#si backups}}, and from backups no later than {{retentionDays}} days after{{/si}}; the Client may export its data beforehand;
9. makes available the information needed to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 and allows reasonable audits, with 30 days' notice.

## 5. End-to-end encryption

The Client acknowledges that the Operator cannot access vault content. Consequently, the Operator can neither search, rectify nor return in plain form data whose keys the Client has lost.

## 7. Liability and duration

This agreement remains in force as long as the Operator processes data for the Client. It is governed by the law of: {{jurisdiction}}.
